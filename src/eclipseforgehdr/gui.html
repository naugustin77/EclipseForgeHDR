Partial convolution

▾
Unsharp masks of the log-stretched image at 2–32 px, added back with their own weights. Each blur runs in polar coordinates, along a streamer rather than across it, and leaves the Moon and the prominences out. Additive, where MGN and NAFE are locally normalised: faint structure stays faint.
The masks are not in this work directory — press Clear cache and Start to build them (adds a few minutes to the stack).

Moon

▾
The lunar disc on its own: its level, the earthshine (maria) contrast, its colour and its own tone curve. The Tone group's curve no longer reaches the disc.

Final sharpening

▾
Last step, on the finished picture. High passes at 1.5–24 px (scaled with the solar radius), combined across the scales as median, minimum and maximum, each blended in soft light at its own strength. The noise gate holds each band back where it is no stronger than the picture’s own grain; 0 = off. The preview follows a moment after the slider.

Settings

▾
Carry a look to another bracket, or send one to somebody else. Settings that describe THIS image rather than the look — the disc radius and margin, the black point, the diamond ring — are written to the file but only applied when it is loaded back into the folder it came from.

Processing recipe

▾
The settings that decide how the data is STACKED — correlation, align filter, tier combine, FNRGF, photometric solve, tiers, merge weight, white balance, photometry, frames, denoise. Kept apart from the look on purpose: a look can be dropped on any bracket and seen at once, while these only mean anything at the next Start. Saved from what the run actually used, not from where the controls happen to be sitting.
