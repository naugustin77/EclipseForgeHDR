Structure

Partial convolution

Unsharp masks of the log-stretched image at 2 to 32 px, ADDED back with their own weights. The blur behind each mask is partial — it is taken in polar coordinates, so it averages along a streamer and not across it, and the Moon and the prominences are left out of it entirely rather than smeared into the corona. Additive and linear, where MGN and NAFE are multiplicative and locally normalised: faint structure stays faint instead of being lifted to the same texture everywhere. Weights 1 / 0.6 / 0.2 / 0.1 are the published starting point. After Jonathan Hill, “Advanced Solar Eclipse Photography”.
The masks are not in this work directory — press Clear cache and Start to build them (adds a few minutes to the stack).

Export

Exports the current view (composite, or the selected detail layer as grayscale). Saved into eclipseforge_output/ inside the raw folder, with a .params.json sidecar.

Settings

Carry a look to another bracket, or send one to somebody else. Settings that describe THIS image rather than the look — the disc radius and margin, the black point, the diamond ring — are written to the file but only applied when it is loaded back into the folder it came from.

Processing recipe

The settings that decide how the data is STACKED — correlation, align filter, tier combine, FNRGF, photometric solve, tiers, merge weight, white balance, photometry, frames, denoise. Kept apart from the look on purpose: a look can be dropped on any bracket and seen at once, while these only mean anything at the next Start. Saved from what the run actually used, not from where the controls happen to be sitting.
